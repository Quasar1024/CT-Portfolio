Project 2 - Data Analysis

This is the page dedicated to showcasing my second project in the class: creating a dashboard for a dataset.

At the start of the term, the focus was on understanding and applying how data can be analysed an visualised to help a reader produce useful insights. To test our knowledge, we were tasked with using Google search data to compile a mock news article. After that, we continued learning about the different processes data goes through in its lifetime, including its gathering, its storage etc.

The last thing we learnt before our big task was how SQL worked, what it was good for, and we even learnt a little bit of it ourselves. Will all of our knowledge in hand, we chose one of a few HUGE practice datasets, full of an overwhelming amount of numerical and categorical data, and used our spreadhseet skills to convert it into a useful dashboard, so that a reader could easily understand the data.

Overall, it seems the reception of this topic was not nearly as positive as the first topic, but who cares. The SQL seemed alright anyway.
